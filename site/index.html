About me

Born in a suburb of Penang, Malaysia; studied architecture at UiTM Shah Alam before switching direction to work on visual storytelling. After several years of freelance work, I travel and explore the world.

Passionate documentary filmmaker with a deep love for storytelling and the power of visual media. With a keen eye for detail and a strong commitment to capturing authentic narratives, I strive to create thought-provoking films that shed light on important social, cultural, and environmental issues.

Experience

Over the years, I have had the privilege of working on a diverse range of documentary projects, each offering unique insights into the human experience. From exploring the lives of marginalized communities to uncovering untold stories from around the world, my work aims to challenge perspectives and foster empathy among viewers.
